Time Vault ($TV) Whitepaper

AI-Powered Tokenized Proof-of-Service Marketplace • v1.0 • July 2026

Abstract

Time Vault introduces a revolutionary AI-powered tokenized marketplace for service hours on Robinhood Chain. By combining smart contract escrow, NFT-based service tokens, and 7 specialized AI agents, Time Vault creates a trustless, intelligent ecosystem where freelance hours become liquid, tradable assets. The $TV token serves as the native utility and governance token powering the entire ecosystem.

The AI Advantage

Unlike traditional freelance platforms, Time Vault deploys 7 specialized AI agents: LYRA (Concierge), VORIAN (Escrow Arbiter), NERIS (Reputation Engine), SOLON (Pricing Oracle), KAIROS (Verification Engine), ATLAS (Talent Scout), and CIRION (Treasury Manager). Together they automate verification, optimize pricing, resolve disputes, and manage treasury, creating the most efficient service marketplace ever built.
