01 · Abstract

Every Hour, Sealed on Chain

Time Vault is the world's first AI-powered tokenized proof-of-service marketplace, a decentralized ecosystem where freelance hours become liquid, tradable NFTs, secured by smart-contract escrow, verified by AI agents, and governed by the community.

By combining ERC-721 Service NFTs, programmatic escrow, and seven specialized AI agents, Time Vault removes the trust deficit that costs the global gig economy billions every year. Buyers purchase verifiable hours; providers build portable, tamper-proof reputations; smart contracts settle instantly when work is proven complete.

"Every Hour, Sealed on Chain."

06 · AI Ecosystem

Seven Specialized AI Agents

Time Vault is not just a marketplace, it is an intelligent ecosystem. Each agent owns one layer of the protocol.

LYRA

AI Concierge & Onboarding
  • Natural-conversation onboarding for new users
  • Guides providers through Service NFT creation
  • 24/7 multilingual support, the face of Time Vault

VORIAN

AI Escrow Arbiter
  • Analyzes work proofs, chat logs, timestamps
  • Objective binding verdicts in seconds
  • Learns from precedent via reinforcement learning

NERIS

AI Reputation Engine
  • Deliverable quality scoring via NLP & computer vision
  • Dynamic, tamper-proof Skill Score
  • Portable reputation across the whole ecosystem

SOLON

AI Pricing Oracle
  • Real-time supply/demand analysis per skill
  • Dynamic pricing recommendations
  • Future demand prediction for providers

KAIROS

AI Verification Engine
  • Validates code, design, writing, consulting
  • Plagiarism & originality detection
  • Confidence Score (0-100%) triggers escrow

ATLAS

AI Talent Scout
  • Vector-embedding skill matching
  • Proactive job alerts for providers
  • Talent discovery for buyers

CIRION

AI Treasury Manager
  • Protocol-owned liquidity optimization
  • Yield strategy & auto-buyback when $TV undervalued
  • Transparent DAO reporting
