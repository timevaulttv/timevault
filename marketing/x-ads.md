# Running Time Vault ads on X

Written after a campaign served zero impressions for a week. Read this before
launching another one.

## The two things that halt a Time Vault ad

**1. A URL in the ad text.** X treats a link typed into the body of a promoted
post as a delivery blocker, not a warning. The destination belongs in the
website card, and the text must not repeat it. This one is fixable in a minute.

**2. Financial Products classification.** X requires pre-authorization for
financial services and cryptocurrency advertising. This is not about wording.
It is about what the account and the landing page are, and `timevault.tv` puts
$TV on the front page. Cleaning up the copy does not clear it, so if the halt
reason mentions financial products, the choices are to apply for the
authorization or to stop trying to promote that destination.

Always read the actual reason on the ad in the Ads tab before changing
anything. Guessing between these two wastes days.

## Copy rules for anything promoted

Organic posts can say whatever is true. Promoted posts get read by a
classifier, and these words are what tripped it:

> escrow, funds, money, payment, locks, releases, invest, returns, yield,
> token, $TV, price, holders, market cap

Nothing in that list is dishonest. It is just what a financial-products
classifier is built to catch. Promote the product, not the asset.

What is left is plenty: freelancers, hours, listings, verification, seven AI
agents that answer live, open source, a public roadmap. All true, all
checkable, none of it financial.

## Ad text that has no URL and no financial language

Pick one. Attach `timevault.tv` as the website card, never in the text.

### A. The agents

> Seven AI agents run this marketplace, and all seven answer questions live.
>
> Ask them what Time Vault does, how a listing works, or what is still being
> built. They will tell you, including the parts that are not finished.

### B. The product

> Freelancers sell hours. Time Vault turns those hours into something a buyer
> can browse, book and verify.
>
> Seven AI agents run the marketplace. You can talk to any of them right now.

### C. Built in the open

> Everything Time Vault builds goes public the day it is built.
>
> The code is on GitHub. The roadmap marks what is done and what is not. There
> is a page that lists what does not exist yet, which is not a page most
> projects publish.

## Checklist before launching

- No URL anywhere in the ad text
- Destination attached as a website card
- None of the words in the list above
- Campaign end date far enough out to matter
- After it goes live, check the Ads tab within an hour. A halt shows up there
  long before the campaign-level suggestions panel notices

## Note on the daily Phase 2 posts

They are organic posts, not ad material. Day 1 is about the escrow contract, so
it is made of exactly the words a classifier rejects. Do not try to promote it.
Run the daily posts organically and spend ad budget on the copy above.
